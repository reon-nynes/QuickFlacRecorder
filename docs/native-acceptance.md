# Native acceptance checks

Run these against the built native app with a connected microphone or interface. Record actual results, the operating system, the audio driver, input device, input stream rate, and any failures in the validation report. These instructions are acceptance procedures, not a claim that all platforms have been checked.

1. On a first launch with no saved settings, confirm a 520 by 300 logical-pixel content area plus native title bar, dark controls, Downloads resolved through the platform's normal location, the normal audio driver/current input, Mono, and input channel 1. Confirm every field and the two-line status fit without overlap. Open the driver, input, and channel popups; check long names, scrolling, Arrow keys, Enter, and Escape.
2. Speak into the selected input before recording. Confirm one mono peak bar responds, its numeric value and scale are in dBFS, and the displayed input stream rate matches the opened device. Fixed output reads 16-bit FLAC. For stereo choose two distinct inputs, send different signals to them, and confirm separately labelled L/R meters respond in that order. A peak at or above full scale shows CLIP briefly. Do not force overload unless your input setup can produce a safe test signal.
3. Edit the recording prefix and choose an existing writable target folder. Confirm the next filename updates. Use `test_01.flac`, `test_09.partial.flac`, and a gap in the folder to confirm prefix `test` previews `test_10.flac`. Existing files must remain unchanged. Remove these fixtures after the check.
4. Enter an unsafe prefix, a nonexistent folder, or an unwritable folder. Confirm an actionable error, disabled Record, no created folder, and no silent fallback. Correct the value and confirm recording becomes available. Long paths and status messages must stay usable through the editable field and full-value tooltip.
5. Record for several seconds, then stop. Confirm the selected input continues metering, elapsed time advances, Record changes to Stop, and target folder, prefix, driver, input, channels, Browse, and Refresh are locked. Confirm `.partial.flac` exists during capture. Confirm finalization shows `Finishing...` with another take disabled, followed by a final `.flac`, a saved status, and the next counter. A short take may finalize between display updates.
6. Make another take. Confirm the recording prefix stays the same and the counter advances. Select the same left and right input in Stereo and confirm the app explains the error and refuses Record. Change to distinct inputs and record stereo.
7. Click the empty meter/status area, then press Space. Confirm Record/Stop toggles. In each text field, Space must enter a space. With each selector focused or its popup open, Space must retain normal selector behavior and never start or stop capture. Check Tab focus and visible focus outlines on all enabled controls.
8. Close the window while recording. Confirm Continue recording and Stop and quit choices. Return/Escape and Continue recording must keep the take running. Stop and quit must finish saving before the process exits. Reopen and inspect the final file.
9. Change the target folder, driver/input, Mono/Stereo channels, and window position; close normally and reopen. Confirm those preferences restore while the recording prefix contains a fresh local launch time. Do not restore the previous prefix.
10. With an input remembered, close the app, disconnect that input, and reopen. Confirm it displays an unavailable input error and refuses Record. It must not silently open a different device. Reconnect or select an available replacement explicitly. Use Refresh if needed.
11. Disconnect the active input during a take. Confirm capture stops with an error, queued audio that can be finalized remains available, and failed capture is not labelled a successful saved take. Inspect any retained `.partial.flac` and the session's reported file. Repeat with device rate changes if supported; the app must stop rather than combine rates in one file. When a supported driver reports an overrun during capture, confirm a specific input-discontinuity/missing-samples error. Overruns from idle metering must not fail a later take. Drivers returning an unsupported overrun count cannot supply this native diagnostic.
12. Leave a `.partial.flac` in the chosen target folder and reopen. Confirm an incomplete-recording status instructs inspection and the matching counter remains occupied. No recovery or automatic deletion should occur.
13. Deny/revoke microphone access in platform privacy settings and reopen. Confirm the app reports permission or input-opening guidance and refuses capture. Grant access, reopen/reselect the input, and confirm idle metering and recording work. Windows, macOS, and Linux permission behavior requires checks on those platforms.

The session test suite supplies controlled filesystem/audio failures, overflow, known mono/stereo samples, channel ordering, FLAC metadata, concurrent reservations, and finalization checks at the accepted RecordingSession boundary. Native checks are needed for focus, device enumeration/disconnection, permissions, window geometry, and preferences.

## Windows microphone check

`RecorderInputCheck` shares `InputController` and `RecordingSession` with the GUI. It records actual platform callback audio and decodes the saved FLAC; it never substitutes generated test audio. Keep the GUI closed if the selected driver requires exclusive device access.

```powershell
.\build\RecorderInputCheck_artefacts\Release\RecorderInputCheck.exe --list-inputs
.\build\RecorderInputCheck_artefacts\Release\RecorderInputCheck.exe --record-check --folder 'D:\existing-folder' --driver 'Windows Audio' --input 'Mic/Line/Inst In 1-2 (UltraLite-mk5)' --channel 1 --duration 8
.\build\RecorderInputCheck_artefacts\Release\RecorderInputCheck.exe --inspect 'D:\existing-folder\microphone-check_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS_01.flac'
```

Use the exact driver/input names printed by `--list-inputs`. The user selected UltraLite-mk5 channel 1 for the local Windows microphone check. Speak during capture. Record the reported device rate before opening, actual input stream rate, FLAC bit depth, channel count, frame count, duration, decoded peak, and decoded RMS. For ASIO, a positive pre-open rate should match the opened rate; zero indicates the device did not expose its existing rate. The tool exits 3 if peak is at or below -60 dBFS, so a silent or low-signal take cannot supply a passing signal check. An observed signal alone does not establish its physical source; listen to the saved take to confirm the intended microphone. Use `--right-channel 2` for a stereo pair and `--prefix <safe-prefix>` for a controlled take name.

macOS and Linux microphone, permission, device hot-plug, default-folder, and preference behavior remain outstanding until someone runs the native checks on those systems. A CI build is insufficient evidence for those behaviors.
